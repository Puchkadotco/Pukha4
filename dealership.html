Dealership / Franchise Policy

Last Updated: 01 January 2026

Thank you for your interest in becoming a Puchka.Co dealership or franchise partner. We aim to build long-term relationships with dedicated entrepreneurs who share our commitment to quality, hygiene, and customer satisfaction.

Important:
Submitting a dealership or franchise enquiry does not guarantee approval. Every application is reviewed individually based on our selection criteria.

1. Eligibility

Applicants should:

  • Be at least 18 years of age.
  • Provide accurate personal and business information.
  • Be willing to follow Puchka.Co operating standards.
  • Have the ability to manage day-to-day business operations.
  • Meet any financial requirements communicated during the application process.

2. Application Process

  • Submit the dealership enquiry form.
  • Our team reviews your application.
  • Eligible applicants may be contacted for further discussion.
  • Location and business feasibility are evaluated.
  • If approved, a formal agreement will be provided.

3. Approval Rights

Puchka.Co reserves the right to approve or reject any dealership or franchise application without providing detailed reasons. Approval depends on business requirements, market potential, and operational considerations.

4. Business Standards

Approved partners are expected to:

  • Maintain high hygiene standards.
  • Use approved ingredients and recipes.
  • Follow branding and marketing guidelines.
  • Deliver quality customer service.
  • Operate in accordance with applicable food safety regulations.

5. Brand Usage

The Puchka.Co name, logo, branding, marketing materials, recipes, and intellectual property remain the exclusive property of Puchka.Co.

No partner may reproduce or modify these materials without written authorization.

6. Fees & Payments

Any dealership or franchise fees, investments, equipment costs, or other payments will be discussed separately and documented in the official agreement.

No payment should be made unless officially requested by Puchka.Co through authorized communication.

7. Confidentiality

Business plans, recipes, pricing strategies, training materials, and operational information shared during the application process are confidential and must not be disclosed without permission.

8. Termination

Puchka.Co reserves the right to suspend or terminate a dealership or franchise agreement if the partner fails to comply with operational standards, legal requirements, or contractual obligations.

9. Limitation of Liability

Puchka.Co shall not be responsible for any business losses, indirect damages, or expenses incurred due to an unsuccessful application or business performance.

10. Changes to this Policy

This Dealership Policy may be updated periodically. Revised versions will be published on this website with the latest update date.
