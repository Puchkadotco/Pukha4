What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer, tablet, or mobile device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences and improve your browsing experience.

1. Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies to:

  • Improve website performance.
  • Remember your preferences.
  • Provide a smoother browsing experience.
  • Understand how visitors use our website.
  • Improve website security.
  • Analyse website traffic.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. Without them, certain features may not work correctly.

Performance Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting anonymous information such as page visits and time spent on pages.

Functional Cookies

These cookies remember your preferences, such as language or previously entered information, to enhance your experience.

Analytics Cookies

We may use services such as Google Analytics to collect anonymous information about website traffic and user behaviour. This helps us improve our website and services.

These services may place their own cookies according to their respective privacy and cookie policies.

4. Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control or disable cookies through browser settings.

You may:

  • Delete existing cookies.
  • Block future cookies.
  • Receive notifications before cookies are stored.

Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality and performance of certain features on our website.
